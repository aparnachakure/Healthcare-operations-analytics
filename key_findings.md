# Key findings from the synthetic dataset

- **1,800 admissions** across **750 patients** were generated for 2025.
- The overall synthetic **readmission rate is 11.72%**. This is a portfolio metric only and has no clinical interpretation.
- **Cardiology** has the highest admission volume at **300 encounters**.
- **2025-03** is the highest-volume month with **155 admissions**.
- **Kidney Disease** has the highest average treatment cost at approximately **₹3,396 per admission** in this synthetic dataset.
- Synthetic insurance coverage represents approximately **72.0% of gross charges**; the remaining amount is out-of-pocket exposure.

These observations are descriptive of the generated portfolio dataset and should not be treated as real hospital benchmarks, clinical evidence, or financial pricing information.
